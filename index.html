About Wisdom-Wave Training Institute

Wisdom-Wave Training Institute is a Namibian education and training provider offering short courses, vocational training, and academic support for learners at different stages of their education.

What we offer

  • Private education from pre-primary to secondary school
  • Grade 1–12 tutoring, homework support, and exam preparation
  • Vocational training, trade testing, and recognition of prior learning
  • Occupational health and safety training and consultancy
  • Certificate and diploma programs and distance learning

How to Study Online

From choosing your course to receiving your certificate.

  1. Enrol and Pay

    Choose a course and follow the payment instructions when you enrol.

  2. Get Portal Access

    Check your email for instructions on signing in to the student portal.

  3. Study and Submit

    Work through the materials, complete quizzes, and submit assignments or video practicals where required.

  4. Receive Your Certificate

    Once you meet the course requirements, check the portal for your certificate.
